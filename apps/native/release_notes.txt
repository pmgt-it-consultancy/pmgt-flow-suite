## What's New

- Added a bounded sync engine that keeps current restaurant operations separate from long-term sales history.
- Older order history can now be loaded explicitly instead of increasing routine synchronization work.
- Added a protected Refresh POS Data option that preserves pending offline work and repairs only the affected local replica.
- Improved Day Closing checks across registered devices, including stale-device retirement and clock-drift safeguards.
- Added durable operation tracking and duplicate-payment protection for interrupted or retried delivery.

## Notes

- The new sync engine is staged behind a feature flag. Existing POS synchronization remains the default until store-level rollout checks pass.
