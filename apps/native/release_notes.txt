## What's New

- Reduced unnecessary data loading across tables, takeout, order history, checkout, and refunds as sales history grows.
- Improved modifier loading for dine-in and takeout, and reduced updates on unchanged cart rows and hidden screens.
- Quantity edits now finish saving before checkout or kitchen send, with retry support when saving fails.
- Reduced database work when adding, updating, and sending orders while preserving totals and discounts.
- Fixed queued sync delivery and interrupted downloads, including large order backlogs.
