## What's New

- Balanced Z-report — the payment breakdown now reconciles with net sales, with a "Total Collected" line so you can confirm the day balances without tallying every transaction.
- Customer receipt setting — option for a minimal customer receipt layout.
- Category sales in reports — product sales are now broken down by category.
- Admin improvements — better filtering and ordering on admin list screens.

## Notes

- Z-report voids are now shown for audit only and are no longer subtracted from net sales again (they were already excluded), so the Sales Summary adds up correctly.
- Refunds record the replacement payment under the original method (cash vs card), keeping the cash/card breakdown accurate.
