## What's New

- Faster order recalculation — order totals now query only the current order's local items, modifiers, and products.
- Modifier-inclusive totals — order, VAT, and SC/PWD discount calculations now include modifier price adjustments consistently.
- More accurate reports — SC/PWD discount rows are recalculated for synced native orders and cleared when discounted items are voided.
- Faster reports — admin report screens avoid unnecessary live subscriptions and date-range reports use bounded indexed queries.

## Notes

- This update corrects accounting for orders with modifiers and SC/PWD discounts.
