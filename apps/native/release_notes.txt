## What's New

- Fixed SC/PWD discount lines showing -₱0.00 in checkout, on receipts, and in reports — discount amounts are now computed at the moment the discount is applied, mirroring the backend formula (20% off VAT-exclusive price for VAT items, 20% off the full price for non-VAT items or non-VAT stores), and include modifier price adjustments in the calculation.

## Notes

- Already-applied discounts on existing open orders will still show ₱0.00 because the server's append-only sync rule blocks back-patching — remove and re-add the discount to refresh the value.
- No backend changes required; the fix lives entirely in the local-first write path.
